\begin{abstract}
We present the Flower-of-Life $\sqrt{3-4i}$ Cantor-Riemann Engine, a generative instrument that produces self-consistent symbolic treatises via fractal sieving, golden-ratio algebraic pinning, and an explicit honesty dial $y'\in[0,1]$. In rigorous mode ($y'=1$) the outputs are heuristics; in symbolic mode ($y'=0$) every statement is declared true inside the closed petal. The engine does not claim classical proofs of open problems.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The boundary between rigorous deduction and creative exploration is usually left implicit. The honesty dial makes it explicit and controllable.

\section{Algebraic Foundation}
The seed is the exact solution of $\sqrt{3-4i}$:
\[
\sqrt{3-4i}=\pm\sqrt{-1+\sqrt{5}}\mp i\frac{2}{\sqrt{-1+\sqrt{5}}}.
\]
These branches supply the frequencies for all Cantor-dust bridges.

\section{Honesty Dial}
When $y'=1$ the engine frames every claim as a generative heuristic. When $y'=0$ the Golden Axiom declares every statement inside the petal to be literal truth. The mode is always printed on the output.

\section{Self-Deconstruction}
The engine can generate a 1000-page treatise that constructs, sieves, and re-embeds its own foundations. This living document is the primary artefact.

\section{Limitations}
No output produced under $y'=1$ constitutes a classical proof of any Millennium Problem. The value of the system is the transparent, inspectable, and extensible instrument it provides for exploration.

\section{Conclusion}
The engine is offered as a new tool for mathematical thought that keeps the distinction between metaphor and proof under explicit control.
